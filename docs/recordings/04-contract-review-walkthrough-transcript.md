**[0:00]** This is Papyrus, a legal contract review desk. Four AI inspectors read every contract, flag what could hurt you, and draft the fix. Nothing leaves without a lawyer's sign-off. This is the whole engagement in one sitting: one contract, one lawyer, start to finish.

**[0:15]** Papyrus starts reading immediately, and the review narrates itself while it runs.

**[0:19]** Six stages: read the document, split it into clauses, then four specialist inspectors read every clause in parallel: law, risk, the firm's own standard, and the money terms. Then negotiation angles, and a plain-English report.

**[0:24]** Several minutes of real reading, shown at ten times speed.

**[1:55]** Keep their wording. Papyrus records that a person saw the flag and chose to keep it. The AI proposes, the human overrules.

**[1:58]** The third flag: unlimited liability. The finding and its redline sit side by side, but here the lawyer likes neither their wording nor the machine's.

**[2:09]** Edit opens the proposed text for rewriting.

**[2:45]** Under the review sits the audit trail: every pipeline step, every inspector finding, and every one of the lawyer's rulings, hash-chained in order.
