# Papyrus, Legal Contract Review: Demo Walkthrough

Runtime 1:39. AI processing shown at 10x; everything else is real time.

**[0:00]** This is Papyrus, a legal contract review system. Four AI inspectors read every contract, flag what could hurt you, and draft the fix. Nothing leaves the desk without a lawyer's sign-off.

**[0:07]** The docket. One contract mid-review, one already signed off. We give it a new one.

**[0:10]** A master services agreement goes in, a Word file, exactly as a client would send it. It never leaves this machine: the model runs on a private GPU lane.

**[0:13]** Papyrus starts reading immediately, and we can watch it work.

**[0:16]** Six stages on the progress bar: pull the text out of the file, split it into clauses, then four specialist checks reading every clause in parallel, law, risk, the firm's own standard, and the money terms. Findings are cross-checked against precedent from past reviews, merged, and turned into concrete redlines.

**[0:22]** Five minutes of reading, shown at 10 times speed.

**[1:00]** The verdict: every clause of the contract on the left, and for each flagged clause a proposal on the right. The clean clauses say so and step aside.

**[1:04]** The first flagged clause, expanded. Each finding comes in three parts: what is wrong, in plain English, what we would change it to, and what happens if you ignore it. Below, the redline diff of their wording against our fix.

**[1:11]** The lawyer agrees, and accepts the proposed wording. That decision is theirs, not the machine's, and it lands on the audit record under their name.

**[1:14]** Next flag. This time the lawyer disagrees with the machine.

**[1:18]** Keep their wording. The AI proposes, the human disposes. Both kinds of call are first-class here.

**[1:21]** And a hard one, where the lawyer wants a second opinion.

**[1:24]** Ask counsel wakes a fifth agent, a senior-counsel reviewer that reads just this clause and rules on it.

**[1:26]** Its ruling comes back attributed and reasoned: escalated, by whom, and why.

**[1:31]** Under it all sits a tamper-evident audit trail: every pipeline step, every inspector finding, and every human decision, hash-chained in order. The lawyer's sign-offs are in the same chain as the machine's work.

**[1:34]** That is Papyrus. AI inspectors do the reading and the drafting, precedent keeps them honest, and a lawyer owns every decision on the record.
